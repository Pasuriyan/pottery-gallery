Visitor Information

Plan Your Studio Visit

We look forward to welcoming you into our showroom and working studio. Here is what to know before arriving.

Transit & Parking — Roads

Situated in the Ceramic District on Artisan Way.hour metered street parking is available directly in front. The 8th Street subway station is a convenient 4-minute walk away. parking also available.

Tactile & Dog Friendly

Pottery is made to be held. You are encouraged to feel weights and glaze textures in our showroom. Well-mannered, leashed pets are warmly welcomed in both the gallery.

Bespoke Consultations

Every pottery piece tells a story. Explore our handcrafted ceramics, discover unique glaze textures, and experience the beauty of artisan craftsmanship in our welcoming showroom.

Accessibility Notice: Our gallery showroom, workshop studio, and restrooms are fully step-free and wheelchair accessible. If you require special assistance, please give us a call ahead of time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about studio visits, custom dinnerware commissions, and safe delivery.

Yes! Our gallery showroom is freely open to the public Tuesday through Sunday. You are welcome to browse our shelves, hold the pieces, and meet resident artisans. If you would like to discuss a custom wedding registry or restaurant tableware commission, we suggest booking an appointment in advance.

Custom pieces generally take 4 to 6 weeks from initial consultation to completion. This timeline ensures proper slow drying (preventing warps/cracks), a 1,800°F bisque firing, custom glaze application, and a final 2,200°F stoneware reduction firing.

We use 100% plastic-free, recyclable honeycomb paper wrap and double-walled cartons. In the rare event an item is damaged during transit, simply send us a photograph within 48 hours and we will replace it or issue a full refund immediately.

Yes, we host private sessions for groups of 4 to 12 guests. These are popular for birthdays, creative retreats, and family gatherings. All materials, wheel throwing guidance, glaze finishing, and kiln firings are included.

All our functional cups, bowls, and plates are glazed with non-toxic, lead-free food-safe minerals and vitrified at high temperatures. While dishwasher safe, we recommend handwashing with gentle soap to preserve unique matte glaze finishes over decades.
