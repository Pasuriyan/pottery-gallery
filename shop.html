Care for Your Ceramics

Made to Be Loved for Years

A few thoughtful habits can help preserve the beauty of your ceramic pieces.

  Gentle Cleaning

Clean with Care

Use a soft sponge and suitable cleaning products. Avoid sudden temperature changes that could damage ceramic pieces.

  Everyday Use

Know Your Ceramic

Check the care instructions for each piece before using it with hot food, a microwave, or a dishwasher.

  Safe Storage

Store Thoughtfully

Avoid stacking delicate ceramics directly against one another. Use protective padding where needed.
